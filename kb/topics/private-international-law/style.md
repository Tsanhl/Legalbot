## First-class checklist (from the marking guide)

- Post-Brexit jurisdiction runs through CPR 6.36–6.37, the PD 6B gateways, a serious issue to be tried, and *Spiliada*. *Vedanta*, *Okpabi* and *FS Cairo v Brownlie* are central for tort claims; engage with Lord Leggatt's dissent in *Brownlie* on how the gateways relate to forum conveniens. Explain why you chose your focus, and give the rationale for recognising judgments (obligation theory, comity, finality). Contrast common-law recognition (*Schibsby*, *Emanuel*, *Adams v Cape*, *Rubin*) with the Hague 2005 and 2019 Conventions, stating scope and temporal limits accurately. Give pinpoints for textbooks.
