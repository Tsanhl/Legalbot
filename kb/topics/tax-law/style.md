# Tax Law: answer rules

## Learned from testing
- Discuss only reliefs and regimes the facts actually engage. Leave out regimes, such as foreign income and gains rules, that nothing in the facts triggers. [2026-11-24 · ref 2026-11-22-1301/q33]
- Check that every conclusion (tax payable, losses carried forward, practical advice) follows from the computation as shown, and fix any mismatch before giving advice. [2026-11-24 · ref 2026-11-22-1301/q33]
- In CGT compliance advice, say whether the client must register for Self Assessment or notify HMRC of chargeability, and by what deadline, taking account of any return already filed. [2026-11-24 · ref 2026-11-24-1510/q33]
- If a filing, notification or payment deadline has already passed on the facts, say so and advise prompt action to limit penalties and interest. [2026-11-24 · ref 2026-11-24-1510/q33]
- When losses are set against gains in a 60-day UK property return, state the timing condition: only losses from earlier tax years, or losses accruing earlier in the same tax year before the property's completion date, can be used. [2026-11-24 · ref 2026-11-24-1510/q33]
