# International Commercial Mediation: answer rules
- State the weight of authority: *Satspace* is a High Court decision, not as binding as a Court of Appeal decision;
  focus on the issues that arise directly from it.
- Push the sources further and give your own view; comparison with the New York Convention and novel, specific reform
  proposals score well. Use a broad range of sources.

## First-class checklist (from the marking guide)

- Benchmark the Singapore Convention against the New York Convention. Analyse Article 4 and the Article 5 refusal grounds, especially Article 5(1)(e)–(f) on mediator misconduct: the undefined seriousness standard, causation, and the clash with confidentiality. Address divergent mediator standards and evidence rules across states. Check the UK's current ratification position. A concrete reform proposal, tested against its costs, and a broad source base (UNCITRAL materials, academic and practitioner commentary) lift an answer.

## Learned from testing
- In problem questions, apply every material fact to each issue it bears on, and link facts that feed more than one ground rather than analysing grounds in isolation. [2026-11-24 · ref 2026-11-22-1301/q25,q26]
