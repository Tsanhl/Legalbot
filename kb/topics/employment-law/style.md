# Employment Law: answer rules

## Learned from testing
- Where an employee lacks the qualifying period for ordinary unfair dismissal, state that they must prove the automatically unfair reason on the balance of probabilities. Keep this separate from the shifting burden of proof for discrimination (Equality Act 2010 s 136, cited only as the source mode allows). [2026-11-24 · ref 2026-11-22-1301/q15,q16]
- In dismissal problems, test every automatically unfair reason the facts engage (eg health and safety, working time, assertion of a statutory right, pregnancy/maternity including redundancy suitable-vacancy priority, whistleblowing). For each, state whether qualifying service is needed and give the consequence and remedy, citing provisions only as the source mode allows and flagging missing ones as gaps. [2026-11-24 · ref 2026-11-22-1301/q15,q16]
- On unilateral changes to terms, explain that the employer may instead end the contract on notice (contractual or statutory minimum, whichever is longer) and offer new terms. State the protections against dismissal and re-engagement as the source mode allows, listing any that are not available as gaps. [2026-11-24 · ref 2026-11-22-1301/q15,q16]
- Where a fact that decides the outcome is missing, flag it, then give the result on each realistic version of that fact rather than stopping at the gap. [2026-11-24 · ref 2026-11-24-1510/q15]
