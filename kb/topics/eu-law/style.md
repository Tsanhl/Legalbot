# EU Internal Market Law: answer rules
- Make the case law named in the title the centre of the essay. On "use" restrictions under Art 34 TFEU: *Commission
  v Italy* (Trailers) — focus on its actual contribution (paras 55 onwards, market access / consumer behaviour) — and
  *Mickelsson and Roos*, assessed carefully; explain what they mean for the evolution of MEQR case law after
  *Dassonville* and *Keck*. QR cases are not relevant to such a question.
- Keep the steps distinct: (1) restriction (MEQR); (2) justification — Art 36 or, depending on the type of measure, the
  Court's mandatory requirements; (3) proportionality, which only arises once a valid justification exists.
- Support "grey area" points with cases, not abstract examples.
- Name cases precisely (*Commission v Italy*, not "*Commission*"); check Advocate General names.
- Citizenship essays: the evolution of the Court's reasoning is the core; analyse relationships between the cases;
  less on secondary legislation (Directive 2004/38); engage with journal articles.
- Free movement of services/advertising: engage with *De Agostini* where the statement concerns it; FMS analysis where
  relevant; be specific.
- No contractions; check grammar and punctuation.
