# Competition Law: answer rules
- Abuse of dominance problem answers: good application of case law is not enough; findings of abuse must also be
  supported by secondary sources — articles, book chapters, Commission guidance and commentary — not case law alone.

## First-class checklist (from the marking guide)

- Frame the answer under the Competition Act 1998 s 18, with Article 102 TFEU case law persuasive under s 60A. Work through market definition, dominance, each abuse, then objective justification (necessity, proportionality, efficiencies). For tying, address separate products, coercion and foreclosure. In digital cases, use *Microsoft*, *Google Android*, *Google Shopping*, *Meta v Bundeskartellamt* (privacy and data as quality) and the effects-based approach after *Intel*. Use current CMA guidance rather than superseded guidance, and mention the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 where relevant. A common weakness: good case law with almost no academic literature, and thin advice to some of the named addressees.
