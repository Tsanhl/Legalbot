# Pensions Law: answer rules
- House style: headings with numbering/lettering (Part I / A / 1 / 1.1 / (a)); short sentences and paragraphs; bold
  defined terms ("**EAD**"); zero SPaG errors; OSCOLA with paragraph pinpoints (without them an answer is unlikely to reach a 2:1 or first).
- Authority order: UKSC > CA > HC > Pensions Ombudsman; cite *Edge* at CA level (Edge CA [50]: the "duty to act
  impartially" is no more than the ordinary duty on a discretionary power-holder). Check cases are not overturned.
- Non-financial factors: start from the scheme's purpose and **PA 2004, s 255** (restrict activities to retirement-benefit
  activities); Law Commission two-part test (no risk of significant financial detriment + members broadly supportive);
  *Palestine* (UKSC) was **3:2** and its remarks on the test are **obiter**, with two formulations (Lord Carnwath: no
  significant risk of financial detriment; Lord Wilson/Law Commission: no risk of significant financial detriment);
  the test derives from *Harries v Church Commissioners* (not a pension trust), contrast *Cowan v Scargill*; Investment
  Regulations 2005; FMLC Report (Feb 2024), APL views, Trust Law International articles. Be critical of UKSC decisions.
- Section 67 PA 1995 does not apply to unregistered schemes (Modification of Schemes Regs 2006, reg 2); consider member
  consent and the actuarial equivalence route; Cost Adjustment Factor as a step in calculation (*KPMG* vs *QinetiQ*).
- Power of amendment restrictions: compare *BBC*, *Lloyds*, *Courage*; Barber/s 62 equalisation; Equality Act 2010 s 67,
  Sch 7 para 5 and the 2010 Exceptions Regs reg 4(2); *For Women Scotland* (UKSC 2025): sex = biological sex.
- Implied duties (*Imperial*, *Braganza*) can be excluded by clear words (cf *Johnson v Unisys*, *USDAW v Tesco*; Re Poole
  contra) but not for maladministration or improper purpose. Consultation Regs 2006 if 50+ GB employees.
- Discretionary death benefits: strongest rules first — power to pay the person at all; improper purpose (*British
  Airways* CA); conflicts (declared interest shifts the burden); Wednesbury only leads to a retaken decision; financial
  interdependence (*Thomas*, *Benge*, *Wild v Smith*); Ombudsman access via reg 1A of the 1996 Regs.
- Identify missing/ambiguous facts and make stated reasonable assumptions.
- Ombudsman citation: *S Mr* [24696] Pensions Ombudsman Determination (30 December 2019).

## First-class checklist (from the marking guide)

- Start from the scheme's purpose and the statutory frame: Pensions Act 2004 s 255, the Investment Regulations 2005, the power of amendment, and Pensions Act 1995 s 67. On non-financial factors, use *Cowan v Scargill*, *Harries*, the Law Commission's two-part test, and the Supreme Court's local government pension scheme decision. Treat that decision critically: it was split, its comments on the test were obiter, the judgments worded the detriment limb differently, and commentators dispute it. On equalisation, be precise about mechanism (*Barber* and its temporal limit, the equal treatment rule, accrued-rights protection). Flag missing facts (registration, consent, actuarial certification, which members were surveyed) and show calculation working.
