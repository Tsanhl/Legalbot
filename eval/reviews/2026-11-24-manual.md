## Recurring patterns found
- Right principle, wrong case, judge or paragraph (e.g. Osborn attributed to Daly; Jackson [159]; AF (No 3) for MB).
- Dissent cited as the holding (Brown: Lord Mustill; Bank Mellat (No 2); Privacy International; Panatown: Lord Millett).
- Consequence of one section attached to another (CA 2006 s 183 to s 177; CRA s 19(14)/(15); Theft Act s 9(3)/(4)).
- Tests stated at the wrong level ("substantial" for "significant" influence; "public interest" for reasonable
  belief; "knowledge" for dishonesty; "at least equal" in Sequana; "readily" for Cream Holdings).
- Outdated law (UCTA s 6(3) repealed; "retained" for assimilated EU law; RDA 1949 old wording; s 21 Housing Act).
- Special protective rules missed (reg 10 priority; Hague 2005 exclusive clauses; consumer jurisdiction; CRA remedies).
- Facts assumed or ignored (BADR holding period; clause wording not given; dates).
- Arithmetic slips carried into the bottom line (CGT computation; contract overpayment).
- Contrary retrieved authority ignored; misquotation inside quotation marks; wrong citation details.
- Procedure stated as optional when mandatory (FDR; small claims mediation); wrong party for a claim (IA ss 238-239).
